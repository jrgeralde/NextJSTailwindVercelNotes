Common Classes
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Class                        | Category                | What it does                                                                      
| ----------------------------| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- 
| `sticky`                     | Positioning             | Makes the element stick to a position when scrolling
| `top-0`                      | Positioning             | Sticks the element to the top (0px from top)              
| Name                     | Z-index                   | Places the element above most other elements   
| `w-[98%]`                 | Width                      | Sets width to 98% of its parent                            
| `mx-auto`                 | Spacing                  | Centers the element horizontally (auto left/right margin)  
| `flex`                        | Layout                     | Enables flexbox layout                                     
| `flex-col`                  | Layout                     | Stacks children vertically (column)                        
| `sm:flex-row`           | Responsive Layout  | Changes layout to horizontal (row) on small screens and up 
| `backdrop-blur-md` | Effects                     | Applies medium blur to background behind the element       
| `bg-white/20`           | Background            | White background with 20% opacity                          
| `border`                   | Border                     | Adds a default border                                      
| `border-white/30`    | Border                      | Sets border color to white with 30% opacity                
| `shadow-lg`            | Effects                      | Adds a large shadow                                        
| `h-auto`                  | Height                       | Height adjusts based on content                            
| `sm:h-24`               | Responsive Height   | Sets height to 6rem (96px) on small screens and up     
| `mt-10`                   | Spacing                    | Adds top margin (2.5rem / 40px)                           
| `rounded-2xl`         | Border Radius          | Applies large rounded corners                              
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rounded Borders
--------------------------------------------------------------
| Class                        | Effect                                                                     
| ----------------------------| ------------------------------- 
| rounded-sm	| Very subtle
| rounded                    | Default slight rounding
| rounded-md	| Medium (what you used)
| rounded-lg	| More rounded
| rounded-xl / 2xl	| Very rounded
| rounded-full	| Fully circular (if square)

div class="flex">
  <div class="w-[10%] bg-gray-200">Left (10%)</div>
  <div class="w-[90%] bg-gray-400">Right (90%)</div>
</div>

Alignment of Flex

flex-row  : main axis is horizontal, alignment is done by justify-, the vertical axis (cross) is aligned by items-
flex-col  : main axis is vertical, alignment is done by justify-, the horizontal axis (cross) is aligned by items-
